CSG Forge

A worker-backed parametric boolean modelling workbench. The upstream three-bvh-csg library lists “Worker Support” on its roadmap and ships src/workers/ as two empty classes — this fork implements it and puts a workbench on top.

upstream gkjohnson/three-bvh-csg · MIT · Evaluator runs unmodified in the worker

Workbench

Parametric Workbench → Modifier chain, five primitives, section plane, four export formats, live worker telemetry.

Upstream examples

SimpleTwo-brush subtraction, the smallest possible case. GeometryResult geometry inspection and attribute layout. HierarchyNested brush transforms and world matrices. HollowShelling via nested subtraction. Multi-materialPer-group material mapping after evaluation. Multi-operationChained boolean operations in one pass. CoplanarCoincident faces — the numerically nasty case. Triangle splitSplitter behaviour on intersecting triangles. DebugBVH and half-edge visualisation helpers.
